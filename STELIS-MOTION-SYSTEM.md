# STELIS MOTION SYSTEM v1

## Philosophy

STELIS motion should feel like:

- Apple
- Porsche
- Rolex
- Tom Ford

Quiet.
Smooth.
Intentional.
Premium.

Never flashy.
Never playful.
Never distracting.

---

## Core Rule

If an animation does not help the owner understand what happened, what changed, or what to do next, it does not belong in STELIS.

---

## Emotional Goal

Every transition should make the owner feel:

- Calm
- In control
- Confident
- Guided
- Safe

---

## Motion Principles

### 1. Never Teleport

Screens, cards, and panels should not appear harshly.

They should enter softly and clearly.

---

### 2. Motion Must Explain

Animation is not decoration.

Motion should explain:

- Where the user is going
- What changed
- What action succeeded
- What needs attention

---

### 3. Fast But Not Rushed

Recommended timing:

150ms = instant response

250ms = normal interaction

400ms = elegant transition

Avoid anything longer unless it is a full screen transition.

---

### 4. Instant Feedback

Every click or tap should respond immediately.

The user should never wonder:

Did it work?

---

### 5. Calm Loading

Never show generic loading text.

Bad:

Loading...

Good:

Preparing Morning Brief...

Analyzing Cash Position...

Reviewing Inventory Risks...

---

## Approved Motion Types

- Gentle fade in
- Soft upward entrance
- Smooth panel expansion
- Subtle button press
- Calm success confirmation
- Progress bar movement
- Number counting for important KPIs

---

## Forbidden Motion Types

- Bouncing
- Spinning logos
- Excessive glowing
- Gaming effects
- Confetti
- Flashing elements
- Random decorative movement

---

## STELIS Timing Scale

Micro interaction:

150ms

Card entrance:

250ms

Panel expansion:

400ms

Full page transition:

400ms to 600ms maximum

---

## STELIS Easing

Motion should feel natural.

Preferred style:

Smooth acceleration

Soft stop

No harsh linear movement.

---

## Product Rule

STELIS does not animate to impress.

STELIS animates to reduce anxiety and increase clarity.
