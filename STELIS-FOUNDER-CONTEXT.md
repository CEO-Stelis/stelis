# STELIS Founder Context

Founder: Marvin

## Vision

The Bloomberg Terminal for Restaurant Owners.

STELIS exists to help owners understand the health of their business in seconds and make better decisions every day.

---

## Core Belief

Owners care about cash first.

Every screen, report, recommendation, and feature should answer:

1. Am I okay?
2. What requires attention?
3. What should I do next?

---

## Priority Order

0. Cash Position

1. STELIS Health Index™

2. Attention Required

3. Today's Decisions

4. Manager Status

5. STELIS Intelligence

6. Deep Dive

---

## Product Philosophy

STELIS is not a reporting system.

STELIS is a Decision Operating System.

The goal is not to show data.

The goal is to help owners make better decisions faster.

---

## Design Philosophy

Apple + Bloomberg + Tom Ford.

Premium.

Simple.

Beautiful.

Reliable.

Intelligent.

Calm.

---

## Golden Ratio Rule

Use Fibonacci / Golden Ratio proportions whenever practical for:

* Layouts
* Typography
* Cards
* Forms
* Spacing
* Visual hierarchy

---

## Color System

Primary Colors:

* Navy
* White

Accent Colors:

* Neon Green (success only)
* Red (risk only)
* Cyan (interaction only)

---

## Luxury Rule

If a color, icon, chart, animation, card, or feature does not help a restaurant owner make a better decision faster, it does not belong in STELIS.

---

## STELIS Health Index™

The STELIS Health Index™ is the primary indicator of restaurant health.

The score should eventually evaluate:

* Financial Health
* Operational Health
* Growth Health
* Leadership Health
* Expansion Health

The index must always explain:

* Why the score exists
* What improved it
* What reduced it
* What action improves it next

---

## Long-Term Goal

An owner should be able to wake up, open STELIS, and understand the state of the entire business in less than 30 seconds.

No stress.

No confusion.

No spreadsheets.

Just clarity and action.
