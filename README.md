# STELIS

The Apple of Restaurant Operating Systems.

## First Dashboard Priorities

When a restaurant owner opens STELIS at 7:00 AM, they should see these 5 things in the first 10 seconds:

1. Sales yesterday, with comparison to the same day last year.
2. Inventory flags or any inventory-related risk.
3. Cash available.
4. Payments due today and this week, including upfront payments and credit payments.
5. AI recommendation.

## Product Philosophy

Premium. Simple. Beautiful. Reliable. Offline-first. AI-powered.

Owners should get answers, not reports.
