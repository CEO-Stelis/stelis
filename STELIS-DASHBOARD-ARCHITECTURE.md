# STELIS DASHBOARD ARCHITECTURE v1

## Core Idea

STELIS is not a dashboard.

STELIS is a Morning Brief.

The owner should open STELIS and understand the business in less than 3 seconds.

---

## Owner Question

The owner is not asking:

What reports do I have?

The owner is asking:

Am I okay?

What can hurt me today?

What should I approve or decide?

---

## Screen Order

The Owner Morning Brief should follow this order:

0. Cash Position
1. Business Health Score
2. Attention Required
3. Today's Decisions
4. Manager Status
5. STELIS Intelligence
6. Deep Dive

---

## 1. Business Health Score

Purpose:

Give the owner immediate confidence or warning.

Example:

Business Health

92 / 100

Status:

Healthy

Includes:

- Sales Health
- Inventory Health
- Cash Health
- Supplier Health
- Waste Health
- Labor Health
- Execution Health

Rules:

- This must be the first major element on the screen.
- It must be readable in under 2 seconds.
- Use green when healthy.
- Use red only when action is required.

---

## 2. Attention Required

Purpose:

Show what can hurt the business.

Maximum 3 items.

Examples:

- Cheese inventory below target.
- BELCA payment due Thursday.
- Waste increased 1.2% yesterday.

Rules:

- Never show more than 3 priority risks.
- The Brain decides what matters most.
- Attention is not the same as action.

---

## 3. Today's Decisions

Purpose:

Show what the owner must approve or decide.

Examples:

- Approve BELCA payment.
- Approve cheese purchase.
- Review breakfast waste issue.

Rules:

- Owner decisions are not manager tasks.
- Decisions should be clear and fast.
- Each decision should explain the expected impact.

---

## 4. Manager Status

Purpose:

Let the owner know if the operation is running without calling anyone.

Examples:

Sabdy: On Track

Stefany: Waiting for Approval

Vivían: Production Complete

Digna: Cash Count Verified

Rules:

- Show status by responsibility.
- Show blockers.
- Do not overwhelm the owner with operational detail.

---

## 5. Chief of Staff

Purpose:

Provide one executive-level insight.

Example:

Revenue is trending 11% above last year.

Biggest Risk:

Cheese inventory.

Biggest Opportunity:

Breakfast traffic continues growing.

Recommendation:

Increase breakfast preparation by 15%.

Rules:

- One paragraph maximum.
- No generic AI text.
- Must help decision-making.

---

## 6. Deep Dive

Purpose:

Allow exploration after the owner understands the business.

Modules:

- Sales
- Inventory
- Cash Flow
- Payments
- Waste
- Labor
- Suppliers

Rules:

- Deep Dive comes after the Morning Brief.
- Details should never appear before decisions.
- No module should be more than 3 clicks away.

---

## Final Rule

The first screen must make the owner feel:

- Calm
- In control
- Ahead of problems
- Confident
- Free to focus on growth

If the screen creates anxiety or confusion, redesign it.
