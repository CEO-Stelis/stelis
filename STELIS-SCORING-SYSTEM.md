# STELIS Health Index™

The STELIS Health Index™ is the primary indicator of restaurant health.

Its purpose is to provide owners with a single trusted measure of the overall condition of the business.

The index ranges from:

0–100

Where:

90–100 = Excellent

80–89 = Strong

70–79 = Stable

60–69 = Attention Required

50–59 = At Risk

0–49 = Critical

The STELIS Health Index™ is calculated from multiple operating dimensions including:

- Cash Health
- Inventory Health
- Operations Health
- Supplier Health
- Waste Health
- Sales Health

The index must always be explainable.

Owners must be able to see:

- Why the score exists
- What increased it
- What decreased it
- What actions improve it

The STELIS Health Index™ is designed to become the restaurant industry's most trusted indicator of business health.
