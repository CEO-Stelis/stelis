Build the Apple of Restaurant Operating Systems.

## Core Principles

- 3-click rule maximum.
- Premium user experience.
- AI-first.
- Offline-first.

- Powerful enough for a 100-store operator.

## First Dashboard

When a restaurant owner opens STELIS at 7:00 AM, they should immediately see:

### 1. Sales Yesterday

- Yesterday sales
- Same day last year
- Difference in dollars
- Difference in %

### 2. Inventory Risks

- Products at risk
- Ingredients running low
- Predicted stockouts

### 3. Cash Available

- Bank balances
- Cash on hand
- Deposits pending

### 4. Payments Due

Today:
- Suppliers
- Payroll
- Rent
- Utilities

This Week:
- Upcoming obligations

### 5. AI Recommendation

One clear recommendation:

"Pay BELCA today."
"Produce 2 recipes of beans."
"Delay non-essential purchases."
"Sales are trending below last year."
